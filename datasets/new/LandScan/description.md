LandScan Global 2024 — ambient population distribution (~1 km/pixel).
30 arc-second resolution. Int32, population counts per cell, nodata=-2147483647.
Very sparse: most pixels are 0 (unpopulated). Heavy-tailed distribution.
5 regions: nyc, iowa, himalayas, sahara, amazon (no ocean).
Source: ORNL DAAC (landscan.ornl.gov) — requires NASA Earthdata login.
