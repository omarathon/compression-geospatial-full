Landsat 8 Collection 1 Level-1 — 6 spectral bands.
UInt16, surface reflectance scaled × 10000 (for B1–B5); B8 is panchromatic DN.
Bands included:
  B1 (coastal/aerosol, 30 m), B2 (blue, 30 m), B3 (green, 30 m),
  B4 (red, 30 m), B5 (NIR, 30 m), B8 (panchromatic, 15 m).
5 regions × 6 bands = 30 files. UTM projection. ~1°×1° footprint per scene.
Source: GCS public bucket (gs://gcp-public-data-landsat), no authentication.
Scene selection: lowest cloud cover in 2021, LC08 (OLI) sensor only.
