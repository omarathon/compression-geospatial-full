ESA WorldCover 2021 — global land cover classification at 10 m resolution.
Byte (UInt8), ~11 discrete class values, nodata=0.
Classes: 10=trees, 20=shrubland, 30=grassland, 40=cropland, 50=built-up,
         60=bare/sparse, 70=snow/ice, 80=water, 90=herbaceous wetland,
         95=mangroves, 100=moss/lichen.
5 regions × 1 tile each = 5 files. WGS84. Tiles are 3°×3° (36000×36000 px).
Source: Microsoft Planetary Computer STAC (collection: esa-worldcover), no authentication.
Sahara region appears nearly uniform (class 60 — bare/sparse vegetation dominates).
