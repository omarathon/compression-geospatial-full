WorldClim 2.1 Total Monthly Precipitation — January (prec_01).
30 arc-second resolution (~1 km/pixel). Int16, values in mm/month, nodata=-32768.
Natively Int16 in WorldClim v2.1 (unlike temperature which is Float32).
5 regions: nyc, iowa, himalayas, sahara, amazon (no ocean — land mask applied).
Source: geodata.ucdavis.edu, no authentication, remote crop via /vsizip//vsicurl/.
