ETOPO 2022 v1 Global Relief Model (NOAA)
15 arc-second resolution (~450 m/pixel at equator) — 4× resolution per axis vs ETOPO1.
Ice surface variant, tiled GeoTIFF. Originally Float32 (metres); converted to Int32
using ×1000 fixed-point quantization (millimetre precision).
6 regions: nyc, iowa, himalayas, sahara, amazon, ocean (North Atlantic).
Source: NOAA NGDC direct wget, no authentication required.
